🧍 When you feel a warning

  • Move away from stairs, roads, water, kitchen.
  • Sit or lie on a soft surface, on your side.
  • Press the alert button to call your contacts.
  • Take medicine exactly as prescribed.

✅ Helpers: DO

  • Stay calm and note the start time.
  • Clear hard or sharp objects; cushion the head.
  • Turn them on their side when jerking stops.
  • Loosen tight clothes; stay until fully awake.

❌ Helpers: DON'T

  • Never put anything in the mouth.
  • Never hold them down.
  • Never give food, water or pills until fully alert.

📞 Call 112 / 108 if

  • Seizure lasts over 5 minutes.
  • Repeated seizures, no waking up between them.
  • Breathing trouble, injury, pregnancy, or first-ever seizure.

🌱 Lifestyle tips

  • Sleep 7–9 hours at fixed times.
  • Never skip medicine; use alarms.
  • Avoid alcohol, screen glare, skipped meals.
  • Manage stress: breathing, walks, yoga.
